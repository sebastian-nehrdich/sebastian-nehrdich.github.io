I presented at the mini-conference "From Palm Leaves to Neural Networks", part of the AI for Ancient Scripts (AI4AS) track at DH2026: Engagement, the annual conference of the Alliance of Digital Humanities Organizations, held in Daejeon, South Korea. The talk covered the capabilities of Dharmamitra, the technical background of multilingual semantic search and retrieval-augmented generation for classical Asian languages, and the shift from "vibe coding" to "vibe philology".
