I led an online workshop for translators hosted by the Tsadra Foundation. The first session introduced MITRA and its current features and capabilities for translation, grammatical analysis, semantic search, and OCR. The second session, moderated together with Sára Csáki-Bertók, was a hands-on review of case studies and material brought by participants.
