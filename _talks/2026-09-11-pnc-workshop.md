I led a hands-on Dharmamitra workshop as ECAI Workshop 04 (Digital Humanities) at the PNC 2026 Annual Conference and Joint Meetings, chaired by Howie Lan (Technical Lead, Electronic Cultural Atlas Initiative, University of California, Berkeley). The workshop covered the Dharmamitra tool overview, MITRA Translate and the grammatical analyzer, MITRA Explore and Deep Research, the MITRA browser extensions, Dharmamitra DB, the MITRA dictionaries, an end-to-end workflow overview, and recent updates.
